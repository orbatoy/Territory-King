How to play

Steer freely

Drag a finger anywhere and you glide smoothly toward it in any direction. Keys: ← → or A / D.

Capture

Swing out, curve back to your colour and everything inside the loop becomes yours.

Stay safe

If anything touches your trail you're out. Cut a rival's trail to knock them out.

Easy: small arena with slower rivals. Medium: big circular arena with 15 rivals. Hard: a giant hexagon 5x bigger than Medium with 25 rivals. The edge is safe in every level: you just slide along it. Rivals patrol the edge and hunt a trail you run along it. Each level saves its own progress and best score.

Anvil

Turns your land into a metal checker plate and makes you invincible for 10 seconds. Rivals that hit you die instead.

Eye

Zooms the view out slightly for 10 seconds while play continues, so you can see more of the arena.

Lightning

Move 40% faster for 10 seconds.

One bonus appears every 30 seconds (3 per game), just off-screen, with an arrow pointing the way. Touch it, or enclose it inside your land, to collect it.
